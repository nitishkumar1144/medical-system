Who We Are

Amayra Medical System is a hospital infrastructure & medical equipment supply company. We specialize in setting up complete medical gas pipeline systems, supplying oxygen cylinders, ICU/OT equipment, and hospital furniture — everything needed to build or upgrade a hospital.

From small nursing homes to large multi-specialty hospitals, we handle end-to-end procurement, delivery and installation, backed by certified products and an experienced technical team.
